Which one wins?

The pointy plane everyone knows, or one with wider wings? Fold both, throw them a few times, and find out.

All you need is some paper and a bit of space. The folding guides are below.

The bad throws count too.

02. time to throw things

  1. Pick a starting line. Use a clear, ground-level space. Same person, same throwing style for both planes.
  2. Throw A, then B. Do that three times. Measure from the line to where each plane first hits the ground.
  3. Keep all six results. Even the one that went straight into the floor. Write down what happened.
  4. Compare the averages. Add a plane’s three distances, then divide by three. Try changing one fold for round two.

03. okay, that didn't fly

It keeps turning to one side.

Compare the wings. Is one folded differently or bent at the edge? Fix one obvious mismatch, then try another throw.

Every throw does something different.

Keep your grip and throwing motion consistent. Check for wind or a nearby fan before changing the plane.

It still won't cooperate.

Try a fresh copy. After enough crashes, a bent nose or loose crease can make the original hard to test.
